# Tools — usage, tests and known limits

Read with `SKILL.md` (Appendix T). All five tools need Python 3.9+ and the standard library only. They work on heuristics: a clean run is a prompt to look, never a substantive pass, and it never substitutes for Gates A–D.

- **`mse_lint.py`** (standard library only): `python3 mse_lint.py manuscript.docx` (also `.md`, `.txt`; `--json` for machine output). Exit 0 = clean, 1 = findings, 2 = unreadable file. Checks: (1) citations in order of first use, no gaps, ranges like [3–5] expanded; (2) every cited [N] has a reference-list entry and every entry is cited — GOST lists (`1. …`) and bracketed lists (`[1] …`) under «Список источников», «Список литературы», «Литература», «Библиографический список» or «References»; (3) decimal points in Russian text; (4) digits glued to Cyrillic units or °C; (5) straight quotes in Russian text; (6) an entry with « // » that has neither pages / an article number nor a DOI or URL — an incomplete record (ГОСТ Р 7.0.100-2018) that Gate A cannot check. It skips the reference list for checks 3–5, English-language lines, the УДК/UDC line, dates, version and licence numbers (v2.2, CC BY 4.0), DOIs, URLs and inline HTML. Also prints the length in characters. Scope: formatting only. A clean lint run is not a substantive pass and never substitutes for Gates A–D. If the linter cannot run, check the same five items by hand.
- **`mse_diff.py`** (standard library only): `python3 mse_diff.py base edited [--forbid "a,b"] [--json]` — changed sentences as a table, NEW NUMBER / NEW CITATION / FORBIDDEN / MISSING NUMBER flags, length change. Exit 1 when anything is flagged.
- **`mse_style.py`** (standard library only): `python3 mse_style.py manuscript.docx [--reference FILE ...] [--impersonal] [--json]` — author voice (not flagged with `--impersonal`, for ГОСТ reports and other impersonal registers), sentence rhythm, semicolons / dashes / parenthetical clauses of four or more words per 1000 words (glosses like «(ЦД)» and «(рис. 1)» are not counted), nominalization density, stock phrases from 2 per 1000 words (Russian канцелярит and stock clichés, English excess style words), contrast frames («не просто X, а Y», "not just X but Y"; three or more), summary tails (two or more paragraphs, and at least 30 %, ending on «Таким образом…» / "Overall, …"), attributions without a citation («исследования показывают», "studies show"), split predicates («провести анализ», "perform an analysis"; three or more), uniform paragraph length (six or more paragraphs with CV < 0.2), abstract–conclusions repetition, repeated openers; the longest sentences to split. The catalogue behind the markers: `references/voice.md`. Exit 1 when flagged. Thresholds are editorial heuristics, not norms; the tool says nothing about who wrote a text. It lists changes; the decision on each one is the editor's (section 1b).
- **`mse_genre.py`** (standard library only): `python3 mse_genre.py manuscript.docx --genre article|review|theses|extended-abstract|grant|report|popsci [--max-chars N] [--json]` — the genre's parts, the moves inside the Introduction and Discussion (cue words), abstract ≤ 250 words and 3–15 keywords (ГОСТ Р 7.0.7-2021), filler openings (ГОСТ Р 7.0.99-2018), empty title openers, hype words, length in characters (pages are checked on the PDF). `grant`: aims, significance, novelty, approach, risks and alternatives, work plan, expected results, numbers. `report` (ГОСТ 7.32-2017): РЕФЕРАТ, СОДЕРЖАНИЕ, ВВЕДЕНИЕ, ЗАКЛЮЧЕНИЕ and the list of sources; in the реферат the volume line, 5–15 keywords (§5.3.2.1) and a length of about one page at most (§5.3.2). `popsci`: the study named, the uncertainty stated, causal wording next to an association, animal or cell results promised to people. Exit 1 when flagged. Cue words are heuristics; the venue's rules win.
- **`mse_grammar.py`** (standard library only): `python3 mse_grammar.py manuscript.docx [--json]` — mechanical language slips in the body text (the reference list is skipped): a word written twice, doubled spaces, a space before or none after a punctuation mark; Russian compound adjectives split by a space; Japanese mixed styles (です・ます / だ・である), mixed 「、」「，」 or 「。」「．」 (section numbers such as «2．» are not counted), full- and half-width digits or Latin letters mixed in one text, doubled particles, ら-less potential forms, wordy forms, double negatives and sentences over 100 characters. Exit 0 = clean, 1 = findings, 2 = unreadable file. A clean run says nothing about agreement, government, commas or word choice (`references/language.md`).
- **Japanese in the other tools:** `mse_genre.py` recognizes Japanese headings (はじめに, 材料と方法, 結果, 考察, 結論, 要旨, キーワード) and cue words, and reports a Japanese abstract in characters (字) without a word limit; `mse_lint.py` finds 参考文献 / 引用文献 and «1)» entries; `mse_style.py` says that its word-based markers do not apply to Japanese (exit 0 with «not applicable» — not a clean result).
- **Tests:** `python3 tests/test_tools.py` — 59 tests on synthetic fixtures (linter, diff, style, genre, regression tests for the v5.1 fixes and for the fixes from the first run on the author's manuscripts, the v5.2 voice markers and applied genres, and the language check in Russian, English and Japanese); run on every push by GitHub Actions.
- **Fixed in v5.1** (each with a regression test): `mse_genre.py` now reads an abstract or keyword list whose label stands on a line of its own, and «следует отметить / подчеркнуть» no longer counts as an implication; `mse_lint.py` no longer reports section, figure and table numbers (§4.10, рис. 1.2, табл. 2.1, п. 3.4) as decimal points; `mse_style.py` no longer counts "US" (the country) as the authorial "us".
- **Added in v5.2** (each with tests): `mse_style.py` — contrast frames, summary tails, attributions without a citation, split predicates, uniform paragraph length, a longer list of stock phrases; `mse_genre.py` — genres `grant`, `report`, `popsci`, and hype words for every genre except `report`.
- **Fixed after the first run on the author's manuscripts** (v5.2, each with a regression test): `mse_genre.py` recognizes numbered headings and headings with a subtitle («1. Введение: …», «8. Выводы и заключение») and flags overclaims («доказывает, что», «гарантирует», «риск … к нулю», "proves that", "guarantees"; negated forms are not flagged); `mse_style.py` no longer counts prepositions, conjunctions and articles as repeated openers, flags stock phrases by density, counts only parenthetical clauses as glue and skips the УДК line; `mse_lint.py` flags incomplete reference records and does not take the slashes of a URL for the « // » separator.
- **Known limits** (open): `mse_grammar.py` needs no dictionary, so it cannot check agreement or government, and its Japanese style check counts only clear sentence endings; Japanese papers that cite as «1)» or with superscripts are not recognized as citing in the Introduction moves of `mse_genre.py`. `mse_genre.py`'s gap cue accepts «но» / "but", so an Introduction without a real gap can pass unflagged; after a keywords label on its own line it takes the next line as the list, whatever it is. `mse_diff.py` matches forbidden terms as substrings, so a short term can match inside a longer word. `mse_style.py` counts every -ция / -ние / -ость word as a nominalization, domain terms included (концентрация, популяция); compare with the author's own texts (`--reference`) before acting on that flag. The v5.2 markers are heuristics; contrast frames, summary tails, split predicates, attributions without a citation and uniform paragraphs did not occur in the two real manuscripts, so they are tested on synthetic text only: a contrast frame or a «таким образом» can be right in context; a citation in any form other than [N], (Author Year) or «et al.» / «и др.» is not recognized, so a cited attribution can still be flagged; the report check does not know which ГОСТ 7.32 elements are mandatory for a given report; presentations, monographs and technical documents have no automatic genre check yet.
