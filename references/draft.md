# Draft mode — full procedure (SKILL.md §1d)

For turning the author's material into a first draft in the target genre. The voice of the text comes from the author; the skill structures, checks and polishes.

1. **Intake** (report §0): genre — journal genres in [`references/genres.md`](genres.md); grants, monographs, research reports, technical documentation, popular science, talk and business presentations, letters around a publication in [`references/genres-applied.md`](genres-applied.md). The call, the funder's form, the publisher's or the customer's rules win; limits, languages, deadline. **Grants:** read the funder's rules first, including any rule on AI tools. Where text drafted with such tools is not accepted as the applicant's own (NIH: applications submitted for the 25 September 2025 receipt date and later, notice NOT-OD-25-132 of 17 July 2025; checked 17 November 2026 — read the current notice before use), do not draft narrative sections: outline, check and edit the author's own text instead (`references/genres-applied.md` §7).
2. **Author's core** ([`assets/outlines.md`](../assets/outlines.md) §0): the main result in one sentence, why it matters, what is new, what is not yet known — in the author's own words; dictation is fine. Without the first three, ask; do not draft.
3. **Material:** data, figures, sources. Every number and citation passes Gates A–B before it enters the text.
4. **Outline** from `assets/outlines.md` (one per genre), for papers with the moves in [`sections.md`](sections.md): the CARS introduction (territory → gap → aim), the Discussion moves (main finding → comparison → explanation → limitations → implications), numbered conclusions. Show the outline and wait for the author's approval.
5. **Draft section by section.** Keep the author's sentences close to verbatim; write the connecting text in the voice of `SKILL.md` §1c, without template echo; mark everything only the author can supply as `[АВТОР: …]`; never invent numbers, results, citations, examples or opinions.
6. **Check:** `python3 mse_genre.py draft.docx --genre …` (article, review, theses, extended-abstract, grant, report, popsci — parts, moves, limits, hype words), Gates A–D, the voice pass (§1c) with `mse_style.py`, and `mse_lint.py`.
7. **Deliver:** the draft, the list of open `[АВТОР]` items, and a short lesson — the three most useful before → after pairs from the author's own material, each with the rule it illustrates, so the author learns the pattern. If the venue's or funder's rules require a statement on tools used to prepare the text, say so once in the delivery note; the wording is the author's, in the venue's form.
