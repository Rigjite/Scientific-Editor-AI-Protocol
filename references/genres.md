# Genres — what each text must do

Read this in `draft` mode (SKILL.md §1d) before building an outline, and in any mode when the genre is in doubt. **The call for papers or the journal's author guidelines always win** over everything below; the figures here are defaults for when the venue says nothing, and each is marked as a standard (ГОСТ) or as common practice.

## 1. Research article (оригинальная статья)

**Job:** report one study so that a specialist can judge it and repeat it.
**Shape:** IMRAD — Introduction, Methods, Results, Discussion (Sollaci & Pereira 2004 trace its adoption); Russian journals often merge «Результаты и обсуждение» and end with numbered «Выводы».
**Front matter (ГОСТ Р 7.0.7-2021, §4.1):** УДК, title, authors and affiliations, аннотация (no more than 250 words, §4.10), ключевые слова (3–15, §4.11) — each in the article's language and then in English; after the text: references, author contributions and conflict-of-interest statement where the venue asks for them.
**Proportions (common practice):** Introduction ≈ 10–15 % of the text, Methods ≈ 15–20 %, Results ≈ 30–35 %, Discussion ≈ 25–30 %, Conclusions short.
**Typical failures:** an introduction that reviews the whole field; Methods that cannot be repeated; Results that interpret; a Discussion that restates the Results; conclusions that repeat the abstract.

## 2. Review article (обзор)

**Job:** organize what is known so that the reader sees the pattern and the gap.
**Shape:** introduction with the question and scope → how the literature was found and selected (databases, dates, terms, inclusion rules; say whether the review is systematic) → synthesis **by theme or by question, never paper by paper** → a comparison table of the evidence → gaps and contradictions → outlook.
**Typical failures:** a list of abstracts; no selection method; claims of completeness without a systematic search; a "conclusion" that only says more research is needed.

## 3. Conference theses (тезисы доклада)

**Job:** carry one message to a programme committee and to listeners in 1–3 pages (common practice; the call for papers sets the exact limit).
**Shape (default, also when there are no headings):**
1. **Актуальность** — one or two sentences: the problem and why it matters now.
2. **Цель** — one sentence.
3. **Материалы и методы** — the object, the period, the main method; a citation only for a non-standard method.
4. **Результаты** — the central result **with numbers**; one table or figure at most, if allowed.
5. **Вывод** — one or two sentences: what follows and for whom.
**Rules of thumb (common practice):** one main message; literature in 1–3 references; no review section; no "в данной работе рассматриваются вопросы…" opening; the title names the result or the object, not a vague theme.
**Typical failures:** a shrunken article with every section and no numbers; a promise of results "to be presented in the talk".

## 4. Extended abstract (EN, for international meetings)

**Job:** let reviewers rank the work on 1–2 pages (common practice).
**Shape:** Background → Aim → Methods → Results (numbers, one figure if allowed) → Conclusion and significance; 3–6 references.
**Typical failures:** background longer than results; no numbers; a conclusion that is a hope.

## 5. Poster text

**Job:** be understood in about a minute from a metre away.
**Shape:** a title that states the main result; three to five short blocks (question, approach, key result with one figure, take-home message); text kept to what supports the figure.
**Typical failures:** a full article pasted into columns; a title that names only the topic.

## 6. Abstract / аннотация (as a separate deliverable)

- **ГОСТ Р 7.0.99-2018:** an аннотация describes the work (about 600 printed characters recommended, §6.8); a реферат conveys its content — subject and goal (only if the title does not make them clear), method (only if new), results, field of application, conclusions — about 850 printed characters (§5.2.12). One paragraph, no headings or lists; start with the main theme; no phrases like «автор статьи рассматривает…»; do not repeat the title (§5.2.3).
- **ГОСТ Р 7.0.7-2021:** in journal articles, no more than 250 words (§4.10).
- In practice most journals want an informative abstract: what was done, what was found (with a number), what it means.
